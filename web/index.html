Know the tableThe rules, at a glance +

The deal

Six decks. Beat the dealer without going over 21. Aces count as 1 or 11; face cards count as 10. The dealer stands on every 17, including soft 17.

Your options

Hit, stand, double on your first two cards, or split identical ranks into up to four hands. Split aces get one more card each and cannot be split again. Late surrender returns half your opening bet.

The payout

An initial, unsplit blackjack pays 3:2. Other wins pay 1:1. A tie returns your wager. Insurance pays 2:1. Opening bet limits apply before play; split and double wagers are additional.

At your own pace

Players share this browser and take turns. Your bankroll limits every wager. You can sit out a round with a $0 bet. Only card ranks are shown; suits do not affect blackjack.
